If your phone was stolen

1Open the official Google or Apple service above and sign in with the account linked to the missing phone.
2Use available options such as secure/lock device, mark as lost, or erase it if appropriate. Do not go to a suspected location yourself.
3Contact your mobile carrier to block the SIM and ask about blocking the device using its IMEI. Report the theft to local police and provide the IMEI and proof of ownership.
4If the phone is offline or switched off, check the official service for a last-known location later. Availability depends on the phone model and settings enabled before it went missing.
